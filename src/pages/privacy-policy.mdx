# TBMQ Privacy Policy

**Revision Date:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") governs the collection, processing, use, disclosure, and protection of personal data by **ThingsBoard, Inc.** ("ThingsBoard," "we," "us," or "our") on the TBMQ website, [tbmq.io](/) (the "Website"), including its documentation, blog and MQTT guides, and in the license checks of TBMQ Professional Edition described in Section 2.3.

ThingsBoard, Inc. acts as the **data controller** for the personal data described in this Policy under applicable global data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable US state privacy legislation.

TBMQ runs on your own infrastructure. Apart from the license checks described in Section 2.3, it sends us no data; the messages and data your MQTT clients exchange stay in your deployment.

The services the Website links to have their own policies:

- The ThingsBoard License Portal ([https://license.thingsboard.io](https://license.thingsboard.io/)), where TBMQ Professional Edition licenses are issued, is covered by the [ThingsBoard Privacy Policy](https://thingsboard.io/legal/privacy-policy/).
- The public TBMQ demo broker at [demo.tbmq.io](https://demo.tbmq.io/) is covered by the [Demo Privacy Policy](/product/privacy-policy/).

The Website is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children under 16; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your visit to the Website and from the license checks of TBMQ Professional Edition, and, with your consent, through the third-party tools described below.

### 2.1 Information Provided Directly by You

- **Contact Form:** When you send us a message through the contact form on our contact page, we receive your first and last name, business email address, company, the subject you chose, your message and the page you sent it from.
- **Documentation Feedback:** When you answer "Was this helpful?" on a documentation page, we receive your rating, the reason you picked, any comment you type and the page address. The feedback form does not ask for your name or email address.

### 2.2 Website Usage, Cookies, and Tracking Technologies

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives your IP address and basic browser data so that the page can load and to keep the Website secure. Analytics and Marketing cookies are set only after you consent in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time under “{PRIVACY_CHOICES_LABEL}” in the site footer. The cookies and similar technologies we use on the Website, and how to manage them, are described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

If you allow Analytics cookies, Google Analytics measures how the Website is used: the pages you view, how you arrived and your browser and device details, linked to a random identifier stored in a cookie.

If you arrive through a link with campaign tags (utm) or a partner referral code (fpr) and you allow Marketing cookies, we store them in your browser and add them to the contact form you submit and to the License Portal sign-up links you follow, so that we know which campaign or partner brought you.

When you submit the contact form, we receive what you enter and the page you sent it from, and, if you allowed the relevant cookies, those campaign tags, the partner code, and the Google Analytics identifier of your browser, so that we can connect your request with how you found us.

Site search is provided by Google Programmable Search Engine and loads only after you allow External media in the cookie banner or press “{SEARCH_CONSENT_LOAD_LABEL}” in the site search; what you type is then sent to Google, and Google sets its own cookies and may show ads in the results.

**Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.** Our Website does not change its behavior in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of Marketing cookies. Analytics and Marketing cookies are set only with your consent in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw that consent at any time under “{PRIVACY_CHOICES_LABEL}” in the site footer. When you consent, Google may collect information about your activity on the Website over time and across other websites, as described in our Cookie Policy.

### 2.3 TBMQ Professional Edition License Checks

TBMQ Community Edition does not contact our servers. TBMQ Professional Edition contacts them to validate its license:

- **Online license:** The first time a broker node starts, it activates with our license server (license.thingsboard.io), sending your license secret, the cluster ID of your deployment and the release date of the software. After that, each node checks its license every hour, sending its instance ID, the cluster ID, the release date, a token returned by the previous check and a retry counter. These checks are required for the license to work.
- **Offline license:** The broker validates the license locally and does not contact the license server. Each node attempts a check-in with our update server (updates.thingsboard.io) at startup and then hourly, sending the customer name, customer ID and subscription ID recorded in the license, the packaging type, and the version of the licensing component. Without outbound connectivity these attempts fail silently and have no effect on the license.

Our servers record the IP address each of these requests comes from, for license-compliance and abuse-protection purposes. Neither kind of license sends usage statistics, MQTT messages, or data about your MQTT clients or users.

Separately, both editions check GitHub (api.github.com) every three hours for the latest TBMQ release, and the TBMQ web interface requests the repository's star count from GitHub directly from the user's browser; no TBMQ data is sent with these requests.

## 4. Third-Party Data Sharing and Processing

ThingsBoard does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share personal and technical data strictly as necessary for the purposes above:

- **Website Tools:** Our Website uses **Google LLC** (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and the Programmable Search Engine that powers our site search), **Cloudflare, Inc.** for hosting, content delivery and protection against automated traffic, and **Formspree, Inc.** (contact form and documentation feedback); these tools set cookies or store data on your device only as described in our Cookie Policy, which also explains how to opt out. For the site search, Google LLC acts as an independent controller under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Each provider's privacy policy is available on its website; the links for the website tools are listed in our Cookie Policy.
- **GitHub:** The page header shows the number of GitHub stars of the TBMQ repository, which your browser requests directly from **GitHub, Inc.** once per visit. GitHub receives your IP address and browser data with that request, as described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); no other data is sent.
- **FirstPromoter:** If you arrived through a partner link and sign up on the License Portal, our servers send FirstPromoter the partner code, your email address and your customer ID, so that the partner can be credited. FirstPromoter sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device on the Website. See [FirstPromoter's privacy policy](https://firstpromoter.com/privacy).
- **Our Sales Team:** Requests you send through the contact form reach our sales team and are kept in our customer relationship management system as described in the [ThingsBoard Privacy Policy](https://thingsboard.io/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **Corporate Transactions:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganization, asset sale, or bankruptcy involving ThingsBoard, Inc., personal data may be transferred to the acquiring or surviving entity subject to standard privacy obligations.
- **Legal and Regulatory Disclosures:** We may disclose personal data where required to do so by applicable law, court order, subpoena, government inquiry, or to protect the safety, legal rights, or property of ThingsBoard, Inc., our users, or the public.

ThingsBoard, Inc. is a United States corporation. Personal data collected from users located outside the US (including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland) is transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States.

To protect cross-border transfers of personal data originating from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, ThingsBoard relies on legally recognized transfer mechanisms, including:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs):** Execution of the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and UK Addendum) for transfers to data controllers or data processors in third countries lacking an adequacy decision.
- **Supplementary Measures:** Supplementary technical, organizational, and contractual measures designed to ensure a level of data protection equivalent to European Union standards.
- **EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework:** Where a provider in the United States is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions, we rely on that certification.

The providers behind the Website are located in the United States (Google, Cloudflare, Formspree, GitHub). You can request a copy of the safeguards we use for a transfer by writing to [privacy@thingsboard.io](mailto:privacy@thingsboard.io).

- **Contact form and documentation feedback** submissions held by our form provider are deleted after 12 months; the copy of a contact request kept by our sales team follows the [ThingsBoard Privacy Policy](https://thingsboard.io/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **Campaign tags and partner code** in your browser: until you withdraw your Marketing consent, it expires after {CONSENT_MAX_AGE_DAYS} days, or you clear your browser data.
- **Your cookie choice:** {CONSENT_MAX_AGE_DAYS} days.
- **License check records:** for as long as the license, the deployment it is used in, or a related legal claim exists.

## 8. Your Data Subject Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, Canada, and various US states), you may hold statutory rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right to Access / Know:** Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy of such data.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your personal data, subject to the retention periods outlined in Section 6.
- **Right to Restrict or Object to Processing:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or request restrictions on specific processing activities.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable copy of personal data provided under contract or consent.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent, withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. For cookies, use “{PRIVACY_CHOICES_LABEL}” in the site footer.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** Lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA) or regulatory body.

To exercise any applicable right, please submit a written request to [privacy@thingsboard.io](mailto:privacy@thingsboard.io). We will respond within statutory timeframes upon verifying your identity and legal authority.
